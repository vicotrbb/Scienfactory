# Scienfactory design

A researcher works at a bright desk with a paper beside their monitor, moving between a question and its evidence. Pure white keeps the daytime workspace quiet; an equivalent charcoal theme supports late sessions.

Use a restrained palette, neutral surfaces, forest-green actions (OKLCH hue 140), strong text contrast, 1px dividers, and 8–12px radii. Inter carries the interface; JetBrains Mono carries source and numerical output. The report preview can use a native serif to distinguish the document from the application.

The shell has a 56px collapsed history rail, a 58px header, left conversation tabs and right artifact tabs. Panels resize with keyboard or pointer and collapse independently. On mobile, switch between conversation and workbench.

Motion lasts 150–220ms and signals opening, selection, streaming, or execution. Reduced motion disables movement. Dialogs trap focus and restore it on close. Every loading and empty state explains the next available action.
