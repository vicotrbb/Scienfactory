# Scienfactory

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, engineers, scientists, and curious people pursuing difficult questions across fields. The primary workflow is a sustained conversation alongside live evidence and executable artifacts.

## Product Purpose

Turn a research question into an inspectable investigation: sources, hypotheses, experiments, formal checks, visualizations, and documented findings. Users bring OpenAI or Anthropic API keys. Tools run in containers. Agents can delegate within explicit resource budgets.

## Brand Personality

Precise, curious, composed. A serious instrument that feels approachable.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered dashboards, decorative agent activity, fabricated citations, and presenting untested claims as verified results. No promotional landing page inside the workspace.

## Design Principles

- Keep conversation and evidence together in independently collapsible, tabbed panels.
- Let the research occupy the screen; keep the history sidebar collapsed initially.
- Show actual tool activity, outputs, failures, and verification scope.
- Make long-running work interruptible and recoverable.
- Keep credentials private and make resource consumption visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Engineering target: WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard operation, focus visibility, descriptive control names, reduced motion, light and dark themes, and a single-panel layout on narrow screens. Formal compliance requires a dedicated audit beyond automated checks.

## Research canvas

The left panel is a conversation, with chronological lead and specialist messages and inspectable tool calls. No prompt suggestion cards. The right panel opens to an empty canvas that agents populate and revise with experiments, code/output, interactive explanations, plots, models, and documents. Files remains a separate tab; Sources, Agents, and Activity remain available. Motion communicates streamed creation, active jobs, and state transitions.
