# Independent validation review

The initial model was revised after review identified a large-displacement regime and an invalid zero-load logarithm in the interactive graph. The corrected run uses 100 N total load.

An independent verifier reconstructed member forces directly from the saved geometry and displacement field. It checked free-DOF equilibrium, support force balance, fixed displacements, positive strain energy, and work-energy consistency. The raw metrics are in independent-verification.json. This checks the mathematical truss model; it does not certify a physical design or account for buckling, yielding, or geometric nonlinearity.

The browser rendered the real Blender GLB and PNG graphs. Agent mistakes and the intentional negative control remain preserved in the research timeline.
